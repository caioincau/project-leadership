## 90-Day Director Readiness Sprint

### Month 1: Foundation
- [ ] Complete self-assessment above
- [ ] Share development plan with manager
- [ ] Identify one cross-team initiative to lead
- [ ] Begin building relationships with 2-3 peer EMs

### Month 2: Execution
- [ ] Launch cross-team initiative
- [ ] Build or implement 2+ playbook artifacts beyond your team
- [ ] Present organizational-level recommendation to leadership
- [ ] Develop one of your tech leads in a specific management skill

### Month 3: Demonstration
- [ ] Show measurable impact from cross-team initiative
- [ ] Get feedback from 3+ stakeholders on your organizational leadership
- [ ] Refine development plan based on quarter's learning
- [ ] Prepare your Director case with evidence from this quarter

---

## Trap Avoidance Checklist

Review monthly:

- [ ] Am I building systems, or am I the system? (Hero trap)
- [ ] Am I developing others' technical judgment, or am I still the technical decision-maker? (Technical depth trap)
- [ ] Am I having the hard conversations, or avoiding them? (Nice manager trap)
- [ ] Am I investing in relationships beyond my team? (Scope limitation trap)
- [ ] Am I building a sustainable track record, or trying to rush? (Impatience trap)
