## Daily AI Workflow

### Morning Review (15 min)

**Inputs:** Overnight PRs, incidents, Slack threads, team metrics

**AI prompt pattern:**
"Summarize the following team activity from the last 24 hours. Highlight:
1. Anything that needs my attention today
2. Any patterns worth monitoring
3. Team members who appear stuck or overloaded
[paste data]"

**Output:** Brief summary with action items for the day

### Meeting Prep (10 min before each meeting)

**For one-on-ones:**
"Given these notes from our last 1:1 and this engineer's recent activity, suggest 3 conversation topics that would be most valuable:
[paste previous 1:1 notes + recent activity summary]"

**For leadership meetings:**
"Synthesize these team metrics into a 3-paragraph executive brief covering: wins, risks, and decisions needed:
[paste metrics]"

**For design reviews:**
"Summarize this technical proposal and identify the 5 most important questions to discuss:
[paste proposal]"

### Communication Drafting (As Needed)

**Process:**
1. Write bullet points of key messages
2. Generate first draft from bullet points
3. Edit for: tone, accuracy, organizational context, personal voice
4. Minimum editing time: 15 minutes per significant communication

**Never skip step 3.** AI-generated communications without editing erode trust.

### Weekly Analysis (30 min, Friday)

**Inputs:** Chapter 4 dashboard data, team velocity, quality metrics, adoption metrics

**AI prompt pattern:**
"Analyze these weekly engineering team metrics. Compare to last 4 weeks. Identify:
1. Improving trends (and likely causes)
2. Declining trends (and suggested investigations)
3. Anomalies worth discussing in next week's 1:1s
[paste data]"

## Ethical Guidelines

### Always Do
- [ ] Edit AI-generated output before sharing (especially reviews and feedback)
- [ ] Be honest if asked about AI involvement in your work
- [ ] Invest time saved by AI in deeper relational work
- [ ] Protect sensitive information (performance data, compensation, personal circumstances)

### Never Do
- [ ] Send unedited AI-generated performance reviews or feedback
- [ ] Share compensation data, HR-sensitive information, or personal circumstances with AI tools
- [ ] Use AI to avoid difficult conversations
- [ ] Present AI-generated analysis as your own insight without verification

### Data Handling

| Data Type | Can Share with AI? | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Team metrics (aggregated) | Yes | No individual names if possible |
| PR/code activity | Yes | Public information |
| Sprint data | Yes | Anonymize if sharing individual data |
| Performance review notes | Conditional | Enterprise tool only, anonymize |
| Compensation data | **NO** | Never share |
| HR-sensitive information | **NO** | Never share |
| Personal circumstances | **NO** | Never share |
